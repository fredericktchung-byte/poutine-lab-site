Identity-as-Code (IaC) Migration

Architecture

Challenge: Manual "click-ops" led to configuration drift and lacked auditability in a large-scale enterprise environment.

Solution: Transitioned Okta configurations to a version-controlled IaC model using Terraform, GitHub, and Claude to automate lifecycle management.

Impact: Established repeatable, auditable deployment pipelines that reduced manual errors and improved security posture.

Enterprise Automation & ROI

Optimization

Challenge: High-volume manual user provisioning and lifecycle management for 7,000+ users were creating significant operational overhead.

Solution: Engineered 150+ Okta Workflows and Tines automations to handle edge cases and common lifecycle events.

Impact: Saved over $2 million in operational costs over 5 years by eliminating manual tickets and increasing system resilience.
